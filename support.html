Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Or follow the steps on the account deletion page.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. Check your inbox (and the Spam folder) for a reset link from Firebase. If nothing arrives within 10 minutes, email us.

The "Continue with Google" button does nothing on Android.

This usually means the device isn't signed into Google Play Services, or the build was installed outside the Play Store. Sign in to Google on the device first. If you're a tester running an APK direct-install, email us your install method and we'll help diagnose.

I weighed my spool and the remaining grams look wrong.

Filamuze tracks net filament weight (filament only, no spool). When entering a weight from a kitchen scale, either type the net weight, or toggle "Weight including spool" in the log-usage dialog and Filamuze will subtract the empty-spool weight automatically. Check that the empty-spool weight on the spool's detail screen matches your real reusable spool.

NFC stickers stopped opening the spool detail.

Re-pair the sticker: open the spool, tap the NFC pill, tap Link NFC, then tap the phone to the sticker again. iOS in particular sometimes loses the NDEF record after a reboot.

I want a feature added.

Email us. We read every request and the roadmap is shaped by what real users ask for.

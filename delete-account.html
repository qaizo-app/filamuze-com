Delete your account

Last updated: 11 May 2026

You can permanently delete your Filamuze account and all data associated with it at any time. There are two paths — the in-app one is instant, the email one is a fallback in case you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 — From inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Filamuze.
  2. Sign in if you're signed out.
  3. Tap Settings (gear icon in the bottom tab bar).
  4. Expand the Account section.
  5. Tap Delete account.
  6. Confirm the deletion.

The account is deleted immediately. You'll be signed out and the app returns to the welcome screen.

Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, send a request to:

support@filamuze.com

Include the email address you registered with. We will verify your identity (a reply from the registered email is usually enough) and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account record (email, sign-in provider, user ID).
  • All your filament spool profiles (brand, material, weight, color, photos, temperatures, prices, notes).
  • All your usage logs (print history, weight changes).
  • All your NFC tag pairings.
  • All photos you uploaded to Firebase Storage.
  • Any custom brands and materials you added.

What we keep

We do not keep a copy of your spool data after deletion. The only things that may remain briefly:

  • Anonymized crash reports already sent to Sentry (the user ID attached to them is your Firebase UID, which itself no longer maps to anyone after deletion). These rotate out automatically within 90 days.
  • Server-side logs from Cloud Firestore / Storage (Google's retention, typically 30 days). These contain no spool content, just access timestamps.

Questions?

Reach us at support@filamuze.com — we read every message.

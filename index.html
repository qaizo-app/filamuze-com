What it does

Built for makers who weigh their spools.

No social feed. No subscription pop-ups. Just the parts you'll actually open every day.

AI label scan

Point the camera at the spool's sticker. Brand, material, color and recommended temps come back filled in.

Weigh by gross or net

Type the kitchen-scale reading directly. The empty-spool weight is subtracted automatically.

NFC tag pairing

Stick a $0.10 NFC tag on the spool. Tap it from anywhere — Filamuze opens to that exact spool.

Running-low reminders

Local notifications when a spool drops below 100 g. Tomorrow morning at 10:00, not mid-print.

Spoolman import

Already running Spoolman? Drop the JSON export — your whole catalog migrates in one tap.

A glance, not a spreadsheet

Every spool, color-coded, weighable.

The main screen filters by material in one tap and the totals at the top recalculate to match what's actually in front of you. Each spool wears its own filament color — no skinned-violet uniformity.

Drying that doesn't lie

Sealed, dried, last touched — at a glance.

Each spool tracks when its bag was opened, when you last ran it through the dryer, and where it sits today. Hygroscopic materials don't surprise you anymore.

Tap. Open. Done.

NFC pairing — a hardware shortcut nobody else has.

Stick a tag on the spool. Tap it whenever you reach for the reel. Filamuze opens to that exact spool's page, ready to log a print. PWA tracking apps can't do this — they have no NFC access.
